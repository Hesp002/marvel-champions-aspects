# Marvel Champions Aspects

Fan reference pages for *Marvel Champions: The Card Game*.

- **[Balanced Aspects](marvel-champions-balanced.html)**: a house assignment of every hero to one aspect. Two aspects have 18 heroes and two have 17, and Deadpool is the only 'Pool hero. Team-up partners are always in different aspects, and every aspect gets at least one hero from each team (Avenger, X-Men, X-Force, Guardian, Web-Warrior, Champion, Defender, Soldier) plus a Mystic. The page includes a team coverage grid and a lock column.
- **[Starter Roster](marvel-champions-roster.html)**: every hero with the aspect their starter deck came in, their hero and alter-ego traits, and their team-up cards.

Both pages are single self-contained HTML files. Open them in a browser, or view them on GitHub Pages. You can sort by any column and filter by aspect, trait or team-up.

Card data comes from [MarvelCDB](https://marvelcdb.com/), with release info from [Hall of Heroes](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/). Marvel Champions is a trademark of Fantasy Flight Games, and Marvel characters are owned by Marvel. This is an unofficial fan project.
